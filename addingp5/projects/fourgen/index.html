☂︎ Princess and Mania

Prologue

✦✧✦

The bodies were lifeless.

Arms and legs twisted in wrong directions, red welts and purple bruises hidden under dark, dark red. Mona fell to the ground, knees landing in the sticky pool of blood.

Eight. Eight people, all dead. Nobles from the regions of Havinon, of Seiena. One the daughter of the Duke of Vasso. Another the son of the Duchess of Meand. All summoned here by the Emperors. All dead now. Before the door to the throne room.

Only seconds ago alive.

Mona’s hands shook as they readjusted their hold on the gun.

Shit, they thought.

“Shit,” they swore aloud, raising an unsteady hand to brush their hair out of their eyes. “Shit.” Revenge was sweet. Sweet like molasses, thick and syrupy and heavy in their throat, so overwhelming they were choking on it. So sweet it made them nauseous. So sweet that they had to bite down on their lip and close their eyes and just focus on fucking breathing, on the sterile lights and grey-ish purple walls around them, on something, anything that would ground them.

You did it, Mona thought to themselves. You did it.

Their gaze dropped to the fish-like eyes of the corpse in front of them.

That had been a person, only moments before.

Do you feel better now?

There were footsteps down the corridor, and they snapped their head up, arms raised and aimed forward, a grim resolution settling in their chest that they would have to do this all once again—only to falter. They were unable to pull the trigger when a woman turned the hall, when they saw her familiar long dress and tiara of wings.

Her eyes widened when she caught sight of Mona, and the carnage that lay between them. “Oh,” Princess Ovira said, her eyes fixed on the shaking gun aimed at her.

Mona wasn’t sure if the ringing in their ears was something she could hear too, as their finger hovered over the trigger. But as the princess glanced away to fully take in the scene in front of her, her shoulders relaxed, her face shifting into something Mona would hesitate to call determination.

With an absurd calmness, she lowered her gaze to look Mona in the eyes. An indigo butterfly landed on her shoulder, wings pulsing in time with their heartbeat.

“Come with me,” said the Princess. “I can get you out of here.”
